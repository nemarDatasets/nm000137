## Additions in Version 1.1.0 (Remaining Four Paradigms, Six Subjects, and Submission Metadata)

Version 1.1.0 expands this deposit from the 17 CLA (`task-imagery`) recordings of 7 subjects to all 75 recordings across 13 subjects (`sub-1` to `sub-13`, corresponding to source aliases SubjectA–F, J, G, H, I, K, L, M) and all five paradigms of the Figshare collection:

- **`task-5F`** (`Kaya2018_5F`): 19 five-finger motor imagery recordings (8 subjects; 13 recordings at 1000 Hz `-HFREQ`, 6 at 200 Hz).
- **`task-HaLT`** (`Kaya2018_HaLT`): 29 hand/leg/tongue motor imagery recordings (12 subjects; 200 Hz).
- **`task-FREEFORM`** (`Kaya2018_FREEFORM`): 3 self-paced real left/right hand key-press recordings (2 subjects; 200 Hz; motor execution, no cue).
- **`task-NoMT`** (`Kaya2018_NoMT`): 7 passive-viewing control recordings (7 subjects; 200 Hz; cue events named `cue_*` so they are distinct from motor imagery).

The 17 existing `task-imagery` (CLA) recordings of `sub-1` to `sub-7` are unchanged. The 58 new source `.mat` files are added under `sourcedata/` with SHA-256 checksums and per-task provenance in `sourcedata/sourcedata_provenance.json`.

## Provenance and Limitations

- All 75 recordings of the figshare collection are included. The description file and the acquisition scripts of the collection are not.
- `task-imagery` (17 recordings) is unchanged from version 1.0.2 (MOABB 1.4.3). The 58 recordings of the four other tasks were added with MOABB 1.8.0 (see `GeneratedBy` in `dataset_description.json`).
- The signals keep the sampling rate of the source files; the conversion applies no filter and no resampling. The 19 EEG channels listed above are kept; the source columns A1, A2 and X3 are not exported.
- `acq_time` in the `scans.tsv` files and the start date in the BDF headers are the placeholder 2018-01-01T00:00:00, not the day of recording. The day of recording is in the name of the source file (`<paradigm>-Subject<X>-<YYMMDD>-...`).
- Not every subject did every paradigm (see the table in the Dataset Overview). `participants.tsv` gives no age, sex or handedness per subject.
- The original MATLAB files are in `sourcedata/`, with their checksums in `sourcedata/sourcedata_provenance.json`.

### Note on `events.tsv` `value` Column

The Paradigm Summary table above lists the original upstream source `.mat` marker codes. In the BIDS `events.tsv` files, the `value` column is assigned alphabetically by `trial_type` name (MNE-BIDS convention), not by source marker code; select trials by `trial_type`.
